# Channel and organizational rules

Read only the section relevant to the asset. Operational limits are dated 2026-10-19; confirm the relevant live page before production/distribution decisions.

## Academic societies and myBYUI

Societies may use temporary artwork up to one year, without integrating the BYU-Idaho name or creating permanent identity. Do not create a society logo. Public marketing promotes awareness and membership; society activities, events, speakers, and announcements are shared with members through **myBYUI Groups**, not general campus promotion. Posters/DSN require Design Conductor approval. Social media is not recommended. National/international chapters contact University Communications before using parent branding. Use the current [Student Groups & Societies page](https://www.byui.edu/branding/student-groups-and-societies) and [society policies](https://www.byui.edu/academic-societies/society-leadership/policies#society-marketing-and-branding), not old I-Belong instructions.

Approved group managers can manage group content; targeted myBYUI messages are coordinated through University Communications. [myBYUI](https://www.byui.edu/branding/mybyui)

## Posters and reserved print

Campus bulletin-board posters are 11 × 17 inches and use Design Conductor approval/branding/printing. Maximum 30 per event/campaign and two per hallway/board area. Use approved boards and push pins, do not cover/remove others' posters, and remove after the stamped expiration date. [Posters](https://www.byui.edu/branding/posters)

Flyers, table tents, mailers, banners, signs, and other reserved print require coordinator contact before design/production. Flyers are distributed at reserved booths or, with permission, department desks; no roaming handouts, unattended public-table stacks, or taping/pinning. Coordinate specifications and production timing with the strategist/printer rather than inventing bleed, stock, or university margin rules. [Print](https://www.byui.edu/branding/print)

## Digital displays

DSN static graphics: **1920 × 1080 JPEG**; keep essential content away from edges and use one simple message. A university logo is **optional**. Video: maximum 30 seconds, recommended under 20 MB, no audio. Use Design Conductor for review/scheduling. Kiosks/stadium billboard are reserved channels. [Digital Displays](https://www.byui.edu/branding/digital-displays)

Campaigns longer than three weeks have a two-weeks-on/one-week-off DSN rotation. [Advertising Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/advertising-guidelines)

## Campus email

Advisory is for necessary/required campus information; The Feed is promotional; standalone Official Announcements require University Communications consideration. Select the smallest appropriate audience and provide a clear next step. Graphics are not mandatory; use Design Conductor email formats when needed. Advisory deadline: Thursday noon for the following Monday; Feed: Tuesday noon for the following Thursday. Confirm live timing before scheduling. [Email](https://www.byui.edu/branding/email)

Advertising Guidelines lists Feed headline/body limits of 100/300 characters including spaces, an optional 175 × 175 graphic, and a URL. Check the live request format before final export. [Advertising Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/advertising-guidelines)

## Social media, paid media, video

University accounts require registration/approval, trained managers, annual training and business-account status. Content needs the appropriate full-time account manager's approval. Review full trend/audio/lyric context and use properly licensed media, including appropriate business-library audio. [Social Media](https://www.byui.edu/branding/social-media-1)

Departments may manage paid ads through approved social accounts; paid search/display/streaming/OTT and other paid platforms are coordinated with University Communications. [Digital Advertising](https://www.byui.edu/branding/digital-advertising)

Department-created videos are limited to department social channels. Other official video uses require University Communications production. Include accurate captions, appropriate rights, and authentic representation. [Video](https://www.byui.edu/branding/video)

## Websites and text messaging

Publish official university information/services through approved platforms. Contact the strategist before an independent web solution; link authoritative existing pages rather than duplicating content. [Website Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/website-guidelines)

Text is a reserved channel with prior approval of type/platform/process and affirmative opt-in outside emergencies. General promotional events, workshops, and activities are not approved for university texts. Emergency communications are managed by Campus Security/University Communications. [Text Messages](https://www.byui.edu/branding/text-messages)

## Merchandise, Viking, IBC, outside organizations

University merchandise uses approved identity and review/production through University Communications and the Store. For event/unit merchandise, practical/strategic color exceptions may apply within review. Keep logos separate from artwork; if space makes branding impractical, consult the strategist about the page's permitted name treatment. Do not make a new identity. [Merchandise](https://www.byui.edu/branding/merchandise-guidelines)

Viking marks are for approved Store merchandise/promotional uses, with review for other uses; do not alter them, form unit identities, or replace primary identity. Mascot appearances have separate setting/approval limits. [Viking Marks](https://www.byui.edu/branding/viking-mark-guidelines), [Mascot](https://www.byui.edu/branding/mascot-guidelines)

IBC businesses use their designated advertising channels and Design Conductor; consult the [IBC page](https://www.byui.edu/branding/ibc-1) for exceptions and limits. Outside-group advertising is generally prohibited, with specified coordinated exceptions and employment/academic opportunities. [Non-University Organizations](https://www.byui.edu/branding/non-university-organizations)

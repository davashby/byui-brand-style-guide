# Visual brand rules

Verified against public sources on 2026-10-19. Use `official-guidance.md` for direct source links and unresolved discrepancies.

## Colors

Brand Blue is Pantone 300, HEX `#006EB6`, RGB 0,110,182, CMYK 89,55,1,0. Lead with blue. Core colors include black `#000000` and white `#FFFFFF`; supporting accents are `#214491`, `#4F9ACF`, `#A0D4ED`, and gray `#949598`. Use a focused palette with accents sparingly. [Color Guide](https://www.byui.edu/branding/color-guide)

Use RGB/HEX for screen work and printer-approved production specifications for print. The website's white RGB row and gray RGB/HEX row conflict: white is 255,255,255; HEX `#949598` decodes to 148,149,152, while the site also lists 148,148,148. Do not silently label competing values equivalent. Use the published HEX for digital design and confirm disputed production values with University Communications.

The general color guide requires consultation before introducing outside colors. Illustration guidance allows purposeful additional colors when needed by the subject/message; merchandise guidance permits practical/strategic exceptions within its review process. Apply the specific medium's guidance and identify any unresolved approval need. An approved color is not automatically readable on every background; check contrast and avoid using color alone to encode information.

## Fonts

Use Arial or Times New Roman for everyday documents, presentations, letterhead, and signatures. Use Acumin or Minion for official public-facing work. Establish hierarchy with size/weight/style rather than extra families. A heading on the site says “Ariel,” but the usage text specifies Arial. Acumin/Minion require licensed Adobe Fonts access; employees can contact IT, and student access depends on qualifying courses/employment. New official materials should use Acumin rather than legacy News Gothic; existing News Gothic materials may continue when necessary during the transition. [Fonts & Typefaces](https://www.byui.edu/branding/fonts-typefaces)

The [Font Use Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/font-use) adds audience distinctions: informal on-campus messages that do not speak for the university may use appropriate fonts; formal campus messages use brand fonts; external informal exceptions require verification; official external messages use brand fonts. Mixed campaigns follow external rules or use separate versions. Resolve ambiguous cases with University Communications.

Before export, inspect actual fonts in retained layouts, theme text, charts, and captions, not only the body paragraphs. Do not assume font files are bundled or that a renderer has the required family. Do not claim licensed-font compliance after silent substitution.

## University and department logos

Use approved university identity for institution-wide communication, or the approved department identity for a college/department/office/program. Do not invent or alter a unit logo. Event/campaign names remain separate. Some campaigns may qualify for custom marks through University Communications; do not rely on old exception lists as automatic permission. [Logos](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logos), [Department Logos](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logos/department)

Use approved files in approved colors. Do not stretch, rotate, crop, redraw, recolor, add elements, or generate official marks with AI. Visible minimum height is 36 px digitally or 3/8 inch in print. Clear space on every side equals the monogram B's height. Measure the visible mark, not a padded image canvas; catalog bounds are estimates for sizing, not official trim boundaries. Approval is required before pairing with another organization's logo. [Official Logo](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logos/official)

The PNG/WebP tiles have solid backgrounds; retain them. Obtain an approved transparent/vector file when that treatment is needed. File-format conversion does not create a vector or authorize editing the artwork. Restricted promotional marks, medallion, seal, legacy wordmark, and anniversary marks require their specified context. Anniversary marks are limited to approved uses during the anniversary period; no end date is inferred. [Other Logos](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logos/other)

## Photography and illustration

Start at the [approved gallery](https://photo.byui.edu/), then coordinate a custom shoot when needed. Use properly licensed stock only when university photography cannot meet the need. Choose authentic, seasonally appropriate, dignified images; preserve composition and meaning and avoid false endorsements. Personal student photographs are not for official university channels. For a temple as the primary subject or living General Authorities, contact BYU-Idaho Copyright. Church Gospel Media is not automatically cleared. [Photography](https://www.byui.edu/branding/photography)

Use uplifting, clean, scholarly illustration appropriate to the message. Additional colors may support the subject; confirm rights. Get permission before recognizable illustrations/silhouettes/caricatures of public figures, General Authorities, university leaders, faculty, or employees. Consult University Communications about temples as the primary subject or Church media. Custom campaign artwork does not replace an approved logo. AI can support exploration, but the creator is responsible for the result. [Illustrations](https://www.byui.edu/branding/illustrations)

Generate artwork with reserved logo space, then place the exact approved logo using deterministic layout/compositing. Do not ask a generative model to recreate the logo or alter it to match a picture.

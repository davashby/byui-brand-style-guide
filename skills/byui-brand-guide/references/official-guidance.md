# Official BYU–Idaho guidance and source notes

Checked 2026-10-13 against live BYU–Idaho pages. These links are the primary sources; consult the live pages before relying on details that may change. This file summarizes the rules rather than reproducing the source pages. Four user-supplied logo image files are bundled in `../assets/` (relative to the skill folder); use the exact color/background variant described in the skill. ## Source hierarchy and reconciled Drive document

The user-provided Google Drive document [BYU-Idaho Branding Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BLA51yxx2ilt44ey4wBz6sopHEZSqkFbE57qoMNJX1s/edit), headed “BYU-Idaho Institutional Brand Architecture and Visual Identity Guidelines,” is a secondary synthesis. Its useful brand architecture framework and claim-by-claim reconciliation are summarized in `institutional-brand-architecture.md`. When wording differs, prefer the current BYU–Idaho website pages linked below. The live site confirms several specific operational details in the Drive document; use the confirmation notes in that reference rather than treating all those details as unverified.

Confirmed examples: the Color Guide specifies Pantone 300 (not the document’s PMS 3005); Writing & Voice allows “BYUI” for limited informal uses (not only internal digital systems); the Advertising Guidelines confirm a 30-second DSN video maximum (not three minutes), 30 posters per event/campaign, maximum two in a hallway/poster-board area, campaign rotation, Design Conductor submission, and Feed character/image limits. The Digital Display Guidelines specify 1920 × 1080 graphics and no audio. The official template page directs staff to approved office, PowerPoint, and email signature templates and the Print and Copy Services business-card system, but does not state the detailed format specs asserted in the Drive document.

## Brand foundations

- [Branding home](https://www.byui.edu/branding/) — entry point, templates, communication services, and links to channel guidance.
- [Logos](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logos) — select the university logo for institution-wide communication, the approved department logo for a college/department/office/program, and limited-use marks only for their approved purposes. Pairings with another organization require contacting University Communications.
- [Official Logo](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logos/official) — approved logo package and usage rules: minimum visible height 3/8 inch in print or 36 px digitally; clear space equal to “B” height; approved colors; no stretching, rotation, cropping, redraw, or added elements; approval before pairing with another organization.
- [Colors](https://www.byui.edu/branding/color-guide) — Brand Blue and core/accent color values and use guidance.
- [Fonts & Typefaces](https://www.byui.edu/branding/fonts-typefaces) — everyday vs. official/public-facing typefaces and use guidance. The displayed “Ariel” heading conflicts with the page copy and FAQ’s “Arial”; this summary follows “Arial” and records the discrepancy.
- [Font Use Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/font-use) — on-campus, off-campus, official, informal, and mixed-audience font distinctions.
- [Writing & Voice](https://www.byui.edu/branding/writing-and-voice) — mission-centered, clear, warm, direct, accessible voice; audience framing; plain-language and inclusive-writing practices; quick reference for general, academic, and Church terminology; and the Written Style Guide as the primary detailed reference.
- [Templates & Resources](https://www.byui.edu/branding/office-templates) — official campus office templates, PowerPoint templates, email signatures, Canva wall-plaque templates, LinkedIn banners, wallpapers, and the Print and Copy Services business-card system. Use the linked source templates; the page does not publish the detailed specifications claimed in the Drive document.

## Asset guidance

- [Photography](https://www.byui.edu/branding/photography) — approved gallery and custom shoots, authentic representation, natural composition, licensing, personal photo restrictions, implied endorsements, temples, living General Authorities, and Church media.
- [Illustrations](https://www.byui.edu/branding/illustrations) — illustration tone, purposeful additional colors when needed, licensing, AI use, recognizable people, Church subject matter, and logo restrictions.
- [Video](https://www.byui.edu/branding/video) — production ownership by channel, accessibility/captions, licensing, and AI use.
- [Viking Mark & Mascot](https://www.byui.edu/branding/viking-marks-and-mascot) — routes to [Viking Mark Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/viking-mark-guidelines), which sets limits on marks and applications.

## Channel guidance

- [Social Media Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/social-media-1) — approved and licensed assets, university/Church standards, and required account review.
- [Website Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/website-guidelines) — approved platforms, accessibility and brand standards, and restrictions on independent official-information sites.
- [Print Materials](https://www.byui.edu/branding/print) — reserved print channels and coordination. For posters, flyers, DSN ads, and Promotional Feed limits, see [Advertising Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/advertising-guidelines) and [Posters](https://www.byui.edu/branding/posters).
- [Digital Display Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/digital-displays) — DSN graphics at 1920 × 1080, no-audio video, approved assets, simple messages, and reserved kiosk/stadium channels. For DSN ad video duration and campaign rotation, see [Advertising Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/advertising-guidelines).

- [Advertising Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/advertising-guidelines) — confirms poster quantities and placement, flyer submission/distribution, DSN 30-second maximum and 2-weeks-on/1-week-off scheduling for campaigns longer than three weeks, and Promotional Feed headline/body/image/URL limits.
- [Posters](https://www.byui.edu/branding/posters) — approved 11 × 17 inch bulletin-board posters, Design Conductor workflow, approved locations, posting/removal rules.
- [Campus Logo Guidelines](https://www.byui.edu/branding/logo-guidelines) — approved graphic elements/artwork, student group/society rules, custom-logo exception categories.

## Help and escalation

The pages list University Communications at [urbranding@byui.edu](mailto:urbranding@byui.edu), (208) 496-2001. When a planned use is restricted, ambiguous, commercial, involves a partner logo, or needs a permission/license decision, direct the user to the relevant official page and recommend contacting University Communications or their Strategic Communications Coordinator.

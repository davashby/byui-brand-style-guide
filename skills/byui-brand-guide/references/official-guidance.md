## Conflicting or dated material

White RGB and gray RGB/HEX entries on the color page disagree; see `brand-rules.md`. The font heading “Ariel” conflicts with Arial in the page text. The PDF writing guide has competing name-dash and time-format statements; see `writing.md`. Some accessibility menu steps reference old Office versions; apply the underlying accessible-output principles with current tools. Do not invent a universal precedence rule for substantive source disagreements: retain the supported default and consult University Communications for the disputed case.
